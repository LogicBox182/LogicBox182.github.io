Who We Are

Wakati Safaris Adventure is a Tanzania-based safari company dedicated to creating exceptional wildlife experiences that go beyond traditional travel. We believe that a safari should be more than just seeing animals — it should be a meaningful journey filled with unforgettable memories, cultural discovery, personalized service, and responsible tourism. With local expertise, passion for wildlife, and deep knowledge of Tanzania’s iconic national parks, we guide travelers through some of Africa’s most breathtaking destinations including Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire, Lake Manyara, and hidden gems across Tanzania. Our mission is to provide authentic safari experiences that leave lasting memories while protecting nature and supporting local communities

Safari experiences are strictly limited to specific times of the year due to weather conditions, wildlife movement, and park accessibility. During rainy seasons, some roads may become difficult to access, and certain camps or lodges may temporarily close. For the best experience, travelers are encouraged to plan their trips early to secure the best safari lodges during the recommended dry seasons when wildlife viewing is at its peak.

The world famous Great Wildebeest Migration typically occurs between June and October, when millions of wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles move across the plains in search of fresh grazing. These months offer some of the most dramatic wildlife encounters, including river crossings and predator action, making them highly sought-after for safari travelers.
